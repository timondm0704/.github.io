## About Me

I analyze genetic and clinical data in collaboration with MaineHealth, and I'm pursuing a PhD in Northeastern's Industry Interdisciplinary PhD program. My research focuses on variance heterogeneity, parent-of-origin effects, and pharmacogenetics.

### My Path
*GIS → Energy → Data Analytics → Biomedical Research*

My path has crossed continents and disciplines: a bachelor's in GIS in China, 11 years analyzing power supply data at a major electric utility, a master's in Applied Data Analytics at Boston University, and now biomedical research. Whatever the domain, the core of the work is the same: building reliable data pipelines, choosing the right statistical approach, and finding the story hidden in large, complex datasets.
